# LastLap

**Fictional brief.** LastLap is not a real business — it is an invented sim
racing lounge and cafe, used as a portfolio exercise in building the kind of
pre-opening page a real local venue would need. The venue, the 1 July 2026
opening date, the Brookfield location, the rigs and the contact details are all
made up. Treat the brand and copy as a design brief, not as facts.

This repo is the **pre-launch landing / waitlist page**, built with Next.js 16.

## Scope

- **Phase 1 (now):** this landing page plus a waitlist that captures email and phone. That is the entire current product.
- **Phase 2 (later, not built yet):** automatic booking and payments on the client side, and an owner/admin platform. Do not build Phase-2 features unless asked. Keep choices future-proof.

## Where to look

- **[docs/engineering.md](docs/engineering.md)** — stack, repo structure, conventions, commands, env vars, deployment, and the waitlist backend.
- **[docs/landing-page.md](docs/landing-page.md)** — the page itself: section structure, design system (color, type, spacing, motion), responsive (mobile / tablet / desktop) choices, and assets.

## Non-negotiables

- Copy voice is plain, human English. No em-dashes, no generic AI filler.
- Keep the dark, editorial design language, and verify any UI change at mobile, tablet, and desktop before calling it done.
